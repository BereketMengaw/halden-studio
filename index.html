The studio

We believe a house should feel like it grew out of its land, and every room inside it should feel like it was made for the people who live there.

Halden is a small team of architects and interior designers. We take on a handful of homes each year, so the people who draw your plans are the same people who choose your stone, your timber and the light over your table.

One studio for the shell and the soul of the house means fewer handovers, fewer compromises, and a home that reads as one idea from the driveway to the linen cupboard.

How we work

Four steps, one team, no surprises.

i.

Listen

We visit the site, learn how you live, and agree on budget and timeline before a single line is drawn.

ii.

Design

Concept sketches, then full plans and 3D views of every room, refined with you until it feels right.

iii.

Build

We manage permits and builders and are on site every week, so what gets built matches what was drawn.

iv.

Furnish

Furniture, lighting and styling land together, and we hand you the keys to a finished home.
