(01) StudioAbout

Houses that fit their land. Rooms that fit their people.

Tikur is a small studio of architects and interior designers. We take on a handful of homes each year, so the people who draw your plans are the same people who choose your stone, your timber and the light over your table.

One studio for the shell and everything inside it means fewer handovers, fewer compromises, and a home that reads as one idea from the gate to the linen cupboard.

(04) ProcessHow we work

Four steps.
No surprises.

1

Listen

We visit the site, learn how you live, and agree on budget and timeline before anything is drawn.

2

Design

Sketches, then full plans and 3D views of every room, refined with you until it feels right.

3

Build

We manage permits and builders and are on site every week, so what gets built matches what was drawn.

4

Furnish

Furniture, lighting and styling land together, and you get the keys to a finished home.
